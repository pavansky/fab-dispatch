# The six views

The tabs across the top all show the **same shift**. Your selection (a job or an engineer) carries
across views, and the URL always reflects what you're looking at, so you can share it.

## Overview

The recommendation, the cost × latency chart, a scorecard per strategy, key findings, the jobs the
strategies disagree on, and a full metric comparison. Start here.

## Floor plan

The fab floor with tool areas, engineers at their home bays and every job. Click a job or an
engineer to inspect it on the right. Switch strategies with the buttons above the map, or choose
**Side by side** to see all five at once. See [Reading the floor plan](help:floor-plan).

## Schedule

A timeline per engineer for the chosen strategy: walking, idle waiting and hands-on work, in time
order. Useful for spotting idle gaps and overloaded engineers.

## Workforce

Demand against certified supply per tool family, the certification matrix, and every job left
unserved with the reason. When a family has unserved work and few top-level engineers, the limit is
**staffing, not the algorithm**.

## Live dispatch

Run the shift in real time: tool-downs arrive as the clock moves, work in progress is locked, and
the plan re-optimises on every event. See [Live dispatch](help:live-dispatch).

## Benchmark

Compare the strategies over many seeded shifts with confidence intervals, and measure each
heuristic's distance from the proven optimum. See [Benchmark and optimality](help:benchmark).

## On a phone or tablet

Everything works by touch. The left panel becomes a **drawer**: tap **☰** (top left) to open the
controls and tap outside to close it. The tabs scroll sideways, the floor plan responds to taps, and
the theme switch moves into the user menu.
