# What-if scenarios

## Generate a shift

In **Shift scenario**, choose a **preset**, a **seed**, and how many engineers and jobs, then click
**Generate shift**. The same seed always produces the same shift, so you can share and reproduce
it. Presets come from your fab's profile, for example *Normal shift*, *Lithography crunch* or
*Excursion / surge*.

## Report a job by tapping the floor

Turn on **Report a job by tapping the floor**, choose its type and when it's reported, then click
inside a tool area. A new job appears there, every strategy re-plans, and the new job opens in the
inspector. Response windows: bottleneck down 45 min, tool down 120 min, PM 240 min.

## Take an engineer off shift

Click an engineer (on the floor plan, schedule or workforce view), then **Take off shift
(what-if)**. Every strategy re-plans without them, so you can see who absorbs their work.
**Restore all** brings everyone back.

## Plan with history-predicted durations

**Plan with history-predicted durations** replaces each tool-down's standard estimate with what
similar past repairs actually took (from repair history), and re-plans. The panel tells you how
many durations changed.
