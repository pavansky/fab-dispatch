# Getting started

Fab Dispatch decides **which equipment engineer goes to which tool-down or preventive maintenance
(PM) job** during a shift, and shows you why. It solves the same shift with five strategies side by
side and recommends one.

## What you see first

The **Overview** opens on a generated shift for your fab: engineers with certifications, and the
jobs that will arrive over the shift. All five strategies solve it in parallel, and the top card
names the **recommended plan** with the reason in one sentence.

## Your first five minutes

1. Read the recommendation card, then look at the **cost × latency** chart beneath it. The orange
   point is the recommended plan.
2. Open **Floor plan** and click any job to see who each strategy sent, and why.
3. In the left panel, drag a **cost weight** (for example *Walking*). Every strategy re-solves.
4. Pick another **preset** (for example *Lithography crunch*) and click **Generate shift**.
5. If you're a dispatcher, open **Live dispatch** and click **Start live shift** to run it in real time.

## Getting help

- Press **?** anywhere to open this help center, or **/** to ask the assistant.
- Look for the **ⓘ** next to a control for help on just that control.
- **Take the tour** in the help menu replays the first-run walkthrough.
