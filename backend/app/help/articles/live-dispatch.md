# Live dispatch

Live dispatch runs the shift as it happens. Tool-downs are revealed only when they occur (no peeking
ahead), work that has started is **locked**, and the chosen strategy **re-plans the open work on
every event**. Everyone watching the shift sees each change as it happens.

## Start a shift

Choose a **Re-dispatch strategy** (ALNS by default) and click **Start live shift**. You need the
**dispatcher** role to start or drive a shift; viewers can join and watch.

## Drive the clock

- **Play** advances the clock continuously at the chosen **speed** (5, 15 or 30 shift-minutes per
  second); **Pause** stops it.
- **+1 h** jumps an hour.
- Only **one dispatcher drives the clock at a time**. The driver holds it for 20 seconds after their
  last tick, and pausing hands it back straight away. The bar shows who is driving.

## Report a bottleneck down

Turn on **Tap floor to report a bottleneck down** and click inside a tool area. The job enters the
shift with a 45-minute response window, the plan re-optimises, and everyone gets an alert.

## Take an engineer off shift

Select an engineer on the floor and click **Take off shift now**. Their open work is re-planned to
others; locked work stays as it is.

## Re-plans don't reshuffle people

Moving a job to a different engineer costs **stability** points, so a re-plan only reassigns work
when it's clearly better. The dispatch log lists what moved.

## Share and rejoin

**Copy link** shares the shift; anyone with access to the fab can open it. **Recent shifts** lists the
fab's shifts so you can **Join** one after closing the tab, and **Leave** returns to the list.

## Connection

The dot next to the clock shows **live** while updates stream in. If the connection drops, the view
says **reconnecting** and catches up automatically, without missing events. While you're offline the
shift keeps its state on the server.
