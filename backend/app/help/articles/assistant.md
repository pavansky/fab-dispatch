# The assistant

**Ask** (bottom right, or press **/**) answers questions about this app and the shift on your screen.

## What it can answer

- **About your shift:** *Why is J006 unassigned?* · *What is E03 doing?* · *Why is PyVRP
  recommended?* · *Compare Greedy and Hungarian.*
- **About the app:** *How do I report a tool-down?* · *What does idle wait mean?* · *Who can run
  benchmarks?*

Answers come with **sources** (help articles) and **actions**, such as opening a job on the floor plan.
Rate any answer with 👍 or 👎; a 👎 lets you say what was wrong, and that's how answers improve.

## Where answers come from

The assistant only answers from two things: these help articles, and the plans computed for the
shift you're looking at. It reads the same constraint engine and results you see on screen. If it
can't find support for an answer, it says so and suggests related articles, rather than guessing.

## Limits

It doesn't change anything: it won't start shifts or move people. It doesn't know about other fabs'
private data or anything outside this app.
